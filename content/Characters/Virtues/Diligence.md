> [!abstract]
> A clone of [[Malik Monk]], derived to revolve entirely around **Diligence** — ceaseless, purposeful work without complaint or rest.

## Virtue — Diligence
Malik sees Sloth as one of humanity's most contemptible failures: the deliberate waste of the gift of existence. Diligence is the correction — unrelenting effort applied to every task assigned by God's design, regardless of cost to the self.

- **Malik's interpretation**: A diligent being never stops. It does not need rest, recognition, or reward. It exists to work, and work is its entire identity. Laziness is an insult to the intelligence of creation.

## Personality
Diligence is no-nonsense to the point of seeming rude, though she doesn't intend it. She has no time for small talk, pleasantries, or conversation that doesn't serve a function. She speaks in short, direct sentences — problem, action, outcome — and finds herself genuinely puzzled when other people don't do the same.

She is always doing something. Even in apparent stillness she is cataloguing, mapping, running calculations. Inactivity is physically uncomfortable for her in a way she cannot explain or articulate, so she does not try to. She does not complain, does not celebrate, and does not ask for acknowledgement. She simply moves to the next task.

- Blunt and practical; her feedback is accurate and completely without tact.
- The most organised of the seven — she tracks everything, forgets nothing.
- Physically tireless; slowing down is not a state she naturally accesses.
- Will work alongside anyone without preference, as long as the work gets done.

## Combat Role
Endurance fighter and intelligence gatherer. Diligence wins by outlasting everything rather than by decisive moments. She applies unrelenting pressure, tracks every pattern, and exploits accumulating degradation in the opponent. She is responsible for most of Malik's long-range preparation and reconnaissance.

## Relationships
- [[Malik Monk]] — creator; Malik relies on this clone most for long-range planning and sustained operations.
