> [!abstract]
> A clone of [[Malik Monk]], derived to revolve entirely around **Humility** — the rejection of pride and the quiet acknowledgement of one's place beneath God.

## Virtue — Humility
In Malik's distorted theology, Humility is the most essential virtue. He believes Pride was humanity's original sin: the moment people decided they knew better than God, they broke the world. Humility means surrendering that illusion of self-importance completely.

- **Malik's interpretation**: True humility is the erasure of self. A being that never places its own desires above God's design can never corrupt the world. Malik views individual ambition as civilisation's root disease.

## Personality
Humility is the quietest of the seven. She speaks only when directly addressed, and even then her sentences are short and self-effacing — more likely to deflect a compliment than accept it. There is no ego to wound, no pride to bruise, and no ambition to appeal to. She refers to herself in the third person when she speaks at all, as though the self she is describing is something slightly removed from her.

She is not passive — she listens with complete attention and remembers everything — but she never makes her presence known unless required. Other people tend to forget she is in the room until she has already done something decisive. She carries herself with the particular stillness of someone who has genuinely stopped caring how they appear to others.

- Does not seek credit, recognition, or acknowledgement.
- Defers to any of the other six in conversation, but not in action.
- Regarded as the most reliable of the seven because she never acts for herself.
- Her presence is grounding; people near her feel the urge to speak less and think more.

## Combat Role
Defensive and support-oriented. Humility does not seek glory in battle — she exists to shield and enable the others. Her IPS output is deliberately suppressed, a living demonstration that restraint is strength. She is the hardest clone to goad into overextending because she has nothing to prove.

## Relationships
- [[Malik Monk]] — creator and source; this clone most closely mirrors Malik's inner ideal.
