> [!abstract]
> A clone of [[Malik Monk]], derived to revolve entirely around **Charity** — the giving of oneself without expectation of return.

## Virtue — Charity
Malik reads Charity not as generosity but as the willing expenditure of self in service of a greater order. Greed, in his view, is the hoarding of what was never yours to begin with — life, power, time. Charity gives all of it back.

- **Malik's interpretation**: The charitable being asks nothing for itself. It is a vessel, not an owner. Every resource it holds exists only to be deployed toward God's corrected world.

## Personality
Charity is the warmest of the seven — not falsely so, but with a straightforward openness that makes her immediately disarming. She asks what others need before thinking of herself, remembers small details about people she has met only briefly, and gives without being asked. This is not a performance; it is simply how she is made.

She is the most outwardly human of the clones and the easiest to be around. People tend to trust her quickly, which she does not exploit — she simply continues giving. Her generosity extends to information, effort, and, in combat, her own safety on behalf of the others.

- Genuinely cheerful, never forced.
- Does not keep score and cannot be leveraged with reciprocity.
- The only clone that actively checks on the others' wellbeing.
- Willing to sacrifice herself for any of the other six without hesitation or complaint.

## Combat Role
Support and resource-transfer specialist. Charity can channel IPS energy to adjacent clones, amplifying their techniques. In direct confrontation she is weaker than most of the others — but she makes every other clone measurably stronger. Enemies who neutralise her first find the remaining six noticeably diminished.

## Relationships
- [[Malik Monk]] — creator; Malik values this clone for its total lack of self-interest.
