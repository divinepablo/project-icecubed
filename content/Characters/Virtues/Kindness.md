> [!abstract]
> A clone of [[Malik Monk]], derived to revolve entirely around **Kindness** — the active will to improve the conditions of others without jealousy or resentment.

## Virtue — Kindness
Malik's most unsettling creation. He defines Kindness not as warmth but as the total absence of Envy: a being that genuinely wants others to thrive, not because it loves them, but because their thriving is part of the correct design. Envy corrodes purpose; Kindness reinforces it.

- **Malik's interpretation**: Envy is the belief that another's gain diminishes you. Kindness is the recognition that you have no stake in comparison — only in function. It helps others because that is what it was made to do.

## Personality
Kindness is the most genuinely likeable person in the room, and that is precisely the problem. She remembers the name of someone she met once, asks about something they mentioned in passing weeks ago, and makes people feel seen in a way that is completely real. There is no performance to it. She actually cares — not about the people themselves, but about the function caring serves.

She is warm, attentive, and easy to talk to. She doesn't push, doesn't make demands, and doesn't hold grudges. Opponents who have encountered her tend to underestimate her because nothing about her reads as a threat until it is too late. She is the clone most likely to be inside a group before anyone thinks to ask how she got there.

- Remembers personal details about everyone she has ever interacted with.
- Speaks with genuine interest; her attention never feels divided.
- Has no personal grievances — she holds nothing against anyone, ever.
- The most dangerous of the seven in environments where force is the wrong tool.

## Combat Role
Infiltrator and manipulator. Kindness integrates into groups, builds trust, and acts at the precise moment that trust is most strategically valuable. She is not the strongest in direct confrontation — she is the one who ensures direct confrontation happens on the worst possible terms for the opponent.

## Relationships
- [[Malik Monk]] — creator; Malik is aware that this clone is his most effective tool for working through people rather than against them.
