> [!abstract]
> A clone of [[Malik Monk]], derived to revolve entirely around **Temperance** — perfect calibration of consumption and output, never more and never less than what the moment requires.

## Virtue — Temperance
Malik views Gluttony as the most structurally destructive vice: the consumption of more than one's role demands. It wastes resources, destabilises systems, and poisons every social structure it touches. Temperance is the precise inverse — exact measurement, exact output, zero excess.

- **Malik's interpretation**: Gluttony is not just eating too much. It is the taking of more power, more land, more life, more attention than your function requires. Temperance takes only what is needed and releases the rest.

## Personality
Temperance is the most economical person in any room. She does not waste words, movement, energy, or time. Her sentences are exactly as long as they need to be — no filler, no pleasantries, no elaboration that wasn't asked for. She is not unfriendly; she simply doesn't see the point in excess of any kind, including conversational excess.

There is something almost mathematical about her. She reads a situation, identifies the minimum effective response, and executes it. She never shows off. She never takes more than her share of anything. She withdraws the instant an objective is achieved, leaving no trace of having been there.

- Speaks in short, complete sentences; never rambles.
- Does not accumulate: no territory, no alliances, no grudges, no possessions.
- Leaves every situation exactly as it needs to be left — no better, no worse.
- The clone least likely to be detected after the fact.

## Combat Role
Efficiency specialist. Temperance ends fights as quickly as possible using the least energy possible. She is immune to attrition strategies — there is simply nothing to drain. She strikes, accomplishes the goal, and disengages. Opponents who expect escalation find she has already left.

## Relationships
- [[Malik Monk]] — creator; Malik respects this clone's perfect economy of action above almost all others.
