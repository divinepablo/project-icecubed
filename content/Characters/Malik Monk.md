# Malik Monk

## Overview
> [!abstract]
> A former monk that has a horribly distorted view of the God after destroying his old monastery.
## Appearance
- **Height**: 6'4 (195 cm)
- **Build**: Imagine Pucci but like better
- **Key Features**:  Darkest tone in the series 

## Personality & Motivation
- **Goal**:  Create a world in his distorted view of God's image. He believes humans deterred from God's intended path and thinks the universe needs to be remade to remove their
%%- **Fear**: %%
- **Flaw**: Not understanding the nuances of human beings

## [[IPS Technique|Abilities]]
- **Heavenly Clone Technique**:
	- Allows the user to create 1:1 perfect clones made in their vision, meaning that if they decide to derive the clone to accentuate a trait then that clone will essentially revolve around this trait.
	- Malik uses this to exemplify his 7 heavenly virtues (see below)
- **Timeline erasure**:
	- To ensure his safety he can erase an ongoing timeline after jumping into a new one (might not make the cut)

<p><strong>Opposes:</strong> Pride &nbsp;|&nbsp; <a href="Virtues/Humility">Full note →</a></p>
<p>The erasure of self. Malik believes Pride was humanity's original deviation from God's design. Humility means acknowledging one's place completely — never placing personal desire above divine order. This clone is Malik's most trusted; it never deviates.</p>
<p><em>Combat role: Defensive and support-oriented. Deliberately suppresses its own IPS output as a living demonstration that restraint is strength.</em></p>
</div>

<div class="tab-content" id="mv-2" role="tabpanel" aria-label="Charity">
<p><strong>Opposes:</strong> Greed &nbsp;|&nbsp; <a href="Virtues/Charity">Full note →</a></p>
<p>The willing expenditure of self. Greed is the hoarding of what was never yours — life, power, time. This clone distributes its resources freely to whichever clone needs them, holds no possessiveness, and will sacrifice itself for any other without hesitation.</p>
<p><em>Combat role: IPS energy transfer to adjacent clones; amplifies every other clone's output at the cost of its own.</em></p>
</div>

<div class="tab-content" id="mv-3" role="tabpanel" aria-label="Chastity">
<p><strong>Opposes:</strong> Lust &nbsp;|&nbsp; <a href="Virtues/Chastity">Full note →</a></p>
<p>The purging of all desire that dilutes intent. Lust is any craving that redirects a being from its assigned role. This clone cannot be manipulated, seduced, or appealed to — it pursues its objective with complete, undistracted efficiency and communicates only in operational terms.</p>
<p><em>Combat role: Assassination and precision strikes. No wasted movement or energy.</em></p>
</div>

<div class="tab-content" id="mv-4" role="tabpanel" aria-label="Diligence">
<p><strong>Opposes:</strong> Sloth &nbsp;|&nbsp; <a href="Virtues/Diligence">Full note →</a></p>
<p>Ceaseless, purposeful work. Laziness is an insult to the intelligence of creation. This clone is never idle — it sustains activity indefinitely and is most responsible for Malik's long-term strategic groundwork. It does not question assignments; it completes them.</p>
<p><em>Combat role: Endurance fighter. Wins by outlasting everything rather than by decisive moments.</em></p>
</div>

<div class="tab-content" id="mv-5" role="tabpanel" aria-label="Patience">
<p><strong>Opposes:</strong> Wrath &nbsp;|&nbsp; <a href="Virtues/Patience">Full note →</a></p>
<p>The absolute refusal to act before the correct moment. Wrath is premature action — responding before God's timing. This clone is impossible to provoke; it absorbs attacks and watches until the precisely correct opening arrives, then acts with absolute conviction.</p>
<p><em>Combat role: Counter-fighter. One step ahead of whatever the opponent just committed to.</em></p>
</div>

<div class="tab-content" id="mv-6" role="tabpanel" aria-label="Kindness">
<p><strong>Opposes:</strong> Envy &nbsp;|&nbsp; <a href="Virtues/Kindness">Full note →</a></p>
<p>The total absence of jealousy or resentment. This clone actively assists whoever it is alongside — including enemies — if that helps the larger plan. It is Malik's most dangerous clone because opponents repeatedly mistake it for an ally before it acts.</p>
<p><em>Combat role: Infiltrator and manipulator. Earns trust and uses it at the most strategically valuable moment.</em></p>
</div>

<div class="tab-content" id="mv-7" role="tabpanel" aria-label="Temperance">
<p><strong>Opposes:</strong> Gluttony &nbsp;|&nbsp; <a href="Virtues/Temperance">Full note →</a></p>
<p>Perfect calibration — never more and never less than what the moment requires. Gluttony is taking more power, land, or attention than one's function demands. This clone takes only what is needed, leaves no excess footprint, and withdraws the instant an objective is achieved.</p>
<p><em>Combat role: Efficiency specialist. Ends fights as quickly as possible using the least energy possible.</em></p>
</div>

## Background
Malik being a former monk from a corrupt monastery that ended up shifting his world view. He noticed the monastery was being controlled by some form of higher ups and chalked up everything he learned to being a lie so they can be controlled and used as weapons of war. Malik then turns to some form of Christianity (due to a this being a fantasy world it'll be derived) and gains a distorted view of the Bible due to his over analyzation and misinterpretations.
Malik hates relying on individuals so he when the he uses/relies on others he forces them into a [[Pacts|pact]]. He intends on recreating the world with his ideal humans and is researching and gathering information on how he can achieve this.
He also has a mental list of human tendencies that he despises
* Untrustworthiness
* Lies and Deceit
* Laziness  
* Greed/Gluttony  
* More cant think atm
