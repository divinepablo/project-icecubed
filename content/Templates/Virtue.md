> [!abstract]
> A clone of [[Malik Monk]], derived to revolve entirely around **Chastity** — the mastery of desire and the preservation of absolute purity of purpose. She presents as a nun.

## Virtue — Chastity
Malik extends Chastity beyond the physical: it is the purging of all desires that dilute intent. Lust, to him, is any craving that redirects a person from their assigned role — lust for pleasure, for validation, for connection. Chastity is singular, undivided focus.

- **Malik's interpretation**: A being that craves nothing cannot be manipulated, seduced, or bought. Chastity is the purity of function — existing only for what one was made to do.

## Personality
Chastity presents as a nun — the only one of the seven with an explicit identity rooted in religious devotion. She wears a habit and carries herself with the unhurried calm of someone who has genuinely made peace with every desire she once had. Her speech is measured and liturgical, unhurried, with a slight cadence that makes ordinary sentences sound like scripture being recited from memory.

She is not cold. She is composed. There is a difference. She will speak with anyone patiently and at length, but she does not get attached, because attachment is the first form of desire. She views every interaction as a passage — something to move through, not something to hold onto.

- Speaks in slow, deliberate sentences; rarely raises her voice.
- Answers questions fully but without elaboration beyond what is necessary.
- Cannot be flattered, threatened, or appealed to through emotion or desire.
- Her stillness reads as serenity to some and as something deeply wrong to others.
- The only clone that prays — she does so in silence, without expectation of reply.

## Combat Role
Precision-strike operative and assassin. Chastity does not waste movement, energy, or action. Every technique is exact. She is the most difficult clone to negotiate with because she has nothing to appeal to — no fear, no ambition, no need. She will complete an objective with the same unhurried thoroughness she brings to everything else.

## Relationships
- [[Malik Monk]] — creator; this clone embodies Malik's ideal of purpose without distraction.
