Starting 350 years ago was the height of [[Imaginary Power System|IPS]] usage. The era lead to the birth of many bloodlines throughout the world.
This era starts with the birth of [[Sun Wukong|the strongest]] [[Imaginary Power System|IPS]] practitioner in history. The era ends soon after the death of [[Sun Wukong|the strongest]] when [[Across D. Way]] freezes all of the main practitioners of [[Imaginary Power System|IPS]], effectively ending the era after 35 long and deadly years.

Information about this era is very skim due to government figures not informing people of their capabilities out of fear of the destruction it can cause.

This golden age of IPS was questionably not harmful to the universe.
