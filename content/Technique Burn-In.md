After a certain amount of uses of a [[IPS Technique]], it gets progressively burned into the user's brain. There will be an equation for this just know its based on actions and energy required

Burnt in techniques can be passed down through genetics as they are practically a part of the person's brain. This allows clans to have passed down techniques that can theoretically be copied by others if fully understood.  

Passed down techniques start at the beginning of the burn in process, essentially once the technique starts losing actions the technique can be passed down hereditably.
