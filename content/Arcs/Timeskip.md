After the tragic passing of the [[Main Character|MC]]'s closest friends due to the hands of [[Malik Monk]], the [[Main Character]] travels to places around the world in search of knowledge about [[Imaginary Power System|IPS]] in hopes of being strong enough to defeat [[Malik Monk]]. Over the 4 years since the [[Death, despair, and more|incident]] at [[Troubled Cavern]] they train physically and learn new techniques while the main character's depression slowly gets worse.
