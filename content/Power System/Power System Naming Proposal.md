## All names will have kanji/hanzi associated with it
 this is so that can be translated to either Chinese or Japanese purely depending on how the series goes name wise

_currently using Japanese names_

| **English Term**           | **Japanese (Romaji)** | **Kanji / Hanzi** | **Lore Justification & Meaning**                                                                                                                             |
| -------------------------- | --------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| [[Imaginary Power System]] | Yuishiki              | 唯識                | "Consciousness-Only" or "Mind-Only." In Buddhism, this refers to the philosophy that all reality is a product of the mind.                                   |
| Practitioners              | Yuishikisha/Shikisha  | 唯識者/識者            | "One who walks the path of Yuishiki." Works as both a title and a common noun while reflecting the power system's title                                      |
| [[IPS Energy]]             | Joriki                | 定力                | "Power of Samadhi" or "Concentration Power." The specific energy cultivated through mental fortitude.                                                        |
| IPS Aura                   | Shikuhi               | 識皮                | "Consciousness Skin." Directly translates to the mechanic of IPS Aura                                                                                        |
| [[IPS Technique]]          | Hō                    | 法                 | "Law" or "Dharma."<br>A technique is not just a "move" it is a law the user imposes on reality.                                                              |
| Mental list of techniques  | Naiteki taizen        | 内的大全              | "Internal Encyclopedia." Refers to the "mental book" where the user recalls their techniques.                                                                |
| [[Technique Burn-In]]      | Kunjū                 | 薫習                | "Perfuming" or "Permeation". This is a real Buddhist term where experiences "scent" the mind and leave seeds (Bijya) that become habits/instincts.           |
