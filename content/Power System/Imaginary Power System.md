_Mainly inspired by Nen and Domain Expansions (very loose)_

## Core Concepts

- literally ANYTHING is possibly in IPS
- IPS is fueled by mental energy and fortitude
- Monks would be the strongest in the verse
- [[IPS Energy]] - the mental energy used to fuel techniques with expandable reserves
- [[IPS Technique]] - a manipulation of [[IPS Energy]] that has to use [[IPS Energy]] from within the body and not the slight amounts around/on the skin
- Using IPS aura ([[IPS Energy]] around the skin) can greatly improve physical capabilities such as speed or strength (not both at once), it can also heal very light cuts (mainly reforms skin instead of healing wounds)
* Durability can be enhanced through either manifesting real things to block through like conjuring [[IPS Technique|techniques]] or manipulating energy around you to soften a blow  
* Super feats in strength and speed can only be achieved through [[IPS Technique|IPS techniques]] (but remember strength or speed at one moment, not both)
